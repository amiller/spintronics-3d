Context: Spintronics (Upper Story, makers of Turing Tumble) is a mechanical kit where chains and sprockets behave like electronic circuits: chain speed = current, chain tension = voltage. Parts: battery (spring motor), resistor (damper), capacitor (torsion spring), inductor (flywheel), junction (differential), diode (ratchet), transistor, switch, button, ammeter, level changer, phonograph. Steve Mould video "Mechanical circuits: electronics without electricity" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QrkiJZKJfpY (2022-10-28).
The official simulator source is in ref/spintronics-simulator/public/ (Phaser 3 + planck.js; parts in parts/*.js, chain in chain.js, main in spintronicssimulator.js). License CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. ref/spintronics-rs is a Rust generator for its save files.
Known from the source: resistor = angular damping ∝ R (resistor-part.js); inductor = fixture density ∝ L, i.e. moment of inertia; capacitor = restoring torque ∝ angle/C; battery/motor = constant torque; transistor = gate sprocket on a spring with joint limits (~6.5°..45°), channel sprocket damping = 100^(2(1-r))-1 where r = gate travel fraction (voltage-controlled brake, MOSFET-like).
